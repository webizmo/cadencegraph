# Demo asset provenance

- `orbit-mark.svg`: original procedural vector artwork created for CadenceGraph. Apache-2.0.
- All composition SVGs and the synthesized demo score: original procedural work, Apache-2.0.
- `fonts/Manrope-Variable.ttf`: Manrope by Mikhail Sharanda and Mirko Velimirovic, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope . License included alongside the font. The original font is unmodified.
- Demo narration text is original. No third-party recordings, voice clones, customer media or paid generative assets are required by the film.

- `demo/garden-original.svg` and `demo/garden-original.png`: original geometric seedling illustration, authored as vector shapes and rasterized with Chromium; Apache-2.0.
- `demo/garden-motion.mp4`: a three-second FFmpeg zoom of that original illustration; H.264 video, no source audio. Apache-2.0 artwork.
- `demo/garden-tone.wav`: three seconds of the original procedural score from `scripts/generate-audio.ts`; an instrumental import fixture, not speech. Apache-2.0.
